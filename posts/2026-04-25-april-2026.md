## New changes to the blog

### Dark mode

It seems that every website has dark mode in some form so I’ve decided to jump on the bandwagon. 

You’ll see a new icon in the top right on the header which will either be a sun or moon icon depending if you’re viewing this blog in light or dark mode. Pressing it will switch the website to the opposite theme. Hooray for technology.

### Comments

This is something that I’ve looked into previously, and went against it because I couldn’t find a solution that I was happy with. There are plenty of plugins that enable comments but are quite pricey. In this implementation, the comments are managed via a Postgres database ([Neon](https://neon.com/)), and whenever someone posts a comment I’ll receive an email (via [resend](https://resend.com/home)).

When it comes to security, there are rules in place which include web links not being allowed, a profanity filter, and I can delete comments if they’re inappropriate. There’s also rate limits in place to prevent spam ([upstash](https://upstash.com/)). 

In short, Neon stores comments, Upstash is the gatekeeper, and the API handler sends it through Resend (which is then sent to me via email).

All I ask is to be nice and don’t post anything you wouldn’t share online. Drop a comment on a post and let me know what you think (I’ll read them!)

## Improvements to the home

Me and my wife decided to get some home improvements done. We decided to work on two areas that had been bugging us the most:

- A backsplash in the kitchen
- An upgraded guest bathroom

My home was a new build when I bought it, and as time has gone on, I’ve slowly realised that the builders cut corners in building my home. From the paint used not being suitable for bathrooms, to tiling being only half complete to cut costs. You begin to notice these things when you live in a home for several years.

After getting in touch with a tradesperson, we got some work done and the difference is incredible. Having the backsplash added made the kitchen look brand new and the bathroom looks unrecognisable after having a shower unit and panels added.

As the improvements began to take shape, me and my wife began discussing what else we could do around the house. We ended up talking about the garden, because at the moment, it’s just a patch of grass with a shed. We may end up buying a grill, and some other garden furniture so we can eat outside when it gets hot in the summer months. Might even attempt to make a smash burger or two.

### Travelling

Paris is a great place to visit, and going by the Eurostar makes it incredibly easy and convenient to get to from London.

My French is getting somewhat better. I’ve found that the best way to get better at the language is to go out and try to speak to French. And trying to speak the language is always appreciated.
