I’m writing this post on Sunday 28th June, all for one reason; it’s the coolest it’s been all week. I had another post in the works (and a video idea that I swear I will get round to recording) but the heat this past week has made it hard to sit down and focus on writing. I’ll get round to that post next month, as it requires a fair amount of research. And I will also start work on that next video (promise!) and get it posted.

In case you missed it, the UK (and much of Europe) has been grappling with a heatwave this week which has the seen the previous record of the hottest day in June broken for [three days in a row](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn4d0n1l8zno) after temperatures reached 37.3C in Suffolk (*that’s 99.14 in Fahrenheit)*. The heatwave led to schools being closed or finishing early, and even train lines advising against all but necessary travel. This heatwave also resulted in me and my wife to have a conversation that I’m sure that many homes in the UK have had this past week; having Air Conditioning installed.

For those who don’t know UK homes, Air Conditioning units are not common, even in new build homes (like my new build home that I bought in 2022, a three bedroom semi-detached home). But I’ve seen over the years that there have been more homes on the estate that I live on that have decided to have Air Conditioning installed. And I won’t lie, I have also been thinking about having it installed, even before this current heatwave. It’s come back into focus more recently since my parents have had it installed in their bedroom and have found that it has made getting through the heatwave more manageable.

I’ll probably get in touch with the company that my parents used to get their Air Conditioning and look to get it installed in two rooms; the lounge and the bedroom. These rooms are the largest and have the most time spent in my house, with the bedroom taking up the whole floor at the top and it being the hottest thanks to heat rising, it makes sense to have air conditioning installed in these rooms. New build homes also have a unique problem, most new homes are designed to trap heat, so in winter you shouldn’t need to rely on turning the heating on as much. But this unfortunately means that in heatwaves, it becomes extremely hot, with the bedroom reaching 37C. 

There is also another reason why; investment. As I mentioned above, homes in the UK don’t normally have AC installed, so if I were to sell my home in the future, having AC installed would help with it standing out on the market. So that would be something else to think about.

So that’s what’s been going on around the UK this week, but here are a few other things that I’ve seen recently:

## Steam Machine

Valve finally unveiled pricing for the Steam Machine, and it’s incredibly expensive. I love the idea of the Steam Machine, but I just think for what you get for the price, it might be better to build your own. Which is now easier than ever as Valve have released SteamOS that can run on your own hardware, as long as you have an AMD GPU.

I might end up building my own Steam Machine and put it in my lounge at some point. Just being able to have my Steam library available on my TV sounds really appealing. But as memory continues to go up in price, I think I’ll be waiting for some time before I can build my own Steam Machine. Meanwhile, Apple have raised the costs of iPads and Macs due to the rising costs of memory and storage.

Investors are becoming concerned about the sustainability over the AI boom, especially as this technology has been hyped up by optimism and now people are wondering if the adoption of AI by corporations can just justify such high prices.

I have said this before and I still stand by it; the next twelve months are going to be crucial for this technology, and how our careers and lives will change. If you haven’t read what I think about Artificial Intelligence and our future, you can read it [here](https://www.joshblewitt.dev/posts/2026-03-26-ai-future). I highly encourage everyone to read it.

It’s actually coming out! And the cover art was revealed!

But, it’s going to cost £69.99 for the standard edition and the retail version of the game does *not* include a disc in the box, which is a bit of a let down. I might be part of the problem as I will be pre-ordering this when I can as the screenshots shown from this game just look *incredible*. I’m looking forward to seeing some gameplay footage of this, just seeing this game run is what will fully convince me to pre-order the game.

## It’s hot

Although it’s much cooler today, it looks like another heatwave is on the way for early July. I don’t think I’ll be able to get Air Conditioning installed by then, so it looks like I’ll be sleeping on the air mattress again very soon.
